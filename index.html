Engineering Philosophy

Deterministic Constraint Solving.
Zero Academic Integrity Compromises.

Standard examination seating generators rely on brittle, non-deterministic random shuffles that cannot be reproduced during audit disputes. Lumora treats examination halls as 8-connected planar graphs, enforcing zero lateral and diagonal paper sightlines through deterministic mulberry32 pseudorandom seeds.

By integrating bin-packing with Ground-Floor PWD front-row priority passes, bipartite invigilator workload matching, and real-time partial delta reallocation, institutions save over 25% in campus HVAC power and eliminate examination hall chaos.
